About Me

My name is Erwin Azucena, I identify as a Bisexual Filipino American who enjoys gaming as a hobby. Currently, I reside in Daly City, but I plan to relocate to Pittsburgh in March. I live with my parents, my aunt, and my older sister, while two of my other older sisters reside elsewhere in California. I have never driven a car in my life because I fear the possibility of being involved in a car accident, which could result in either my death or damage to the vehicle I would be operating. My swimming skills are not very strong, so when I visit the pool, I tend to either sit in a distant corner or use a floatation device to avoid the risk of drowning. I often find myself alone since everyone around me is preoccupied with their own responsibilities, leading me to spend time at the mall, playing video games on my devices, or watching movies and shows on my television. I have a tendency to be quite active, maintaining the hyper energy I had as a child, which suggests that I may have ADHD or Autism. Finally, I am a lowkey fan of Hatsune Miku, owning several of her figures, listening to her music whenever possible on my computer or phone, and playing the games in which she appears, whether they are collaborations or her own titles.
